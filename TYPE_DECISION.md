# Public Type Decision

The public type is `@mgreten/observation-record` at `2026.08.23.1`.

`observation-record` names the durable artifact rather than its author, organization,
intended audience, or a particular decision domain. This is an intentional incompatible
replacement for `@mgreten/founder-marketing-observation`: the prior type's role- and
domain-specific vocabulary is not retained as an alternate public type or compatibility
alias.

The record remains evidence-preserving and recommendation-only. It does not validate a
claim or grant decision, execution, publication, communication, provider, network, or
spending authority.
